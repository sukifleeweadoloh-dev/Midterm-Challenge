  • Introduction;

    Palestine, officially the State of Palestine,is a country in the Southern Levant region of West Asia. It encompasses the West Bank, including East Jerusalem, and the Gaza Strip, both of which areoccupied by Israel. These territories are collectively known as the Palestinian territories, or occupied Palestinian territory.

  • Palestinian Governorates;

    Palestine is divided into 16 governorates, 11 in the West Bank and 5 in the Gaza Strip. The governorates are further divided into municipalities, village councils, and refugee camps. The governorates are listed below, along with their populations, areas, regions, and locations.
